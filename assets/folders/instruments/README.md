# instruments

Cultural props and instruments for lively interiors.

Esta carpeta contiene 10 assets deterministas. Cada subcarpeta documenta sus previews, sprite sheets, manifest y variantes.

## Ejemplos

- [lute](./lute/README.md): lute prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [flute](./flute/README.md): flute prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [drum](./drum/README.md): drum prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [harp](./harp/README.md): harp prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [horn](./horn/README.md): horn prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [bell tower](./bell-tower/README.md): bell tower prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [ocarina](./ocarina/README.md): ocarina prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [panpipes](./panpipes/README.md): panpipes prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [maraca](./maraca/README.md): maraca prop for taverns, churches and festivals.
- [music box](./music-box/README.md): music box prop for taverns, churches and festivals.

## Uso

Busca por categoría con `get_asset_library`, resuelve un item con `get_asset_library_item` y aplica las variantes con los algoritmos del MCP sobre una copia del asset.
